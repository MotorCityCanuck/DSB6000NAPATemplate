# Outputs Guide

**Purpose:** This document explains how the outputs folder can organize team-generated analytical artifacts, supporting evidence, and deliverables for the NAPA Olympic Analytics Platform. Teams determine their Gold output organization, what to retain, and how to summarize it for review.

## Subfolder Purposes

- `validation/`: quality-check summaries, reconciliation evidence, and other validation artifacts.
- `scorecards/`: small review-ready exports or summaries related to team-designed scorecards.
- `tournament_candidates/`: candidate-selection outputs or summaries generated by the team.
- `figures/`: screenshots, charts, or lightweight presentation visuals.

National player rankings are not a mandatory deliverable. Final recommendation and presentation requirements are governed by the NAPA RFP and Canvas assignments; this directory does not prescribe Gold tables, output filenames, metrics, scoring methods, models, or ranking algorithms.

## Commit Guidance

Large generated outputs should not be committed unless specifically required and small enough for review. Prefer small summary exports, screenshots, or clearly labeled evidence artifacts when appropriate.
