# Assignment Context

**Purpose:** This team-maintained worksheet records how the consulting team interprets the NAPA Olympic Analytics case, plans its work, and identifies questions requiring clarification. It does not replace or reproduce the official assignment.

The NAPA RFP and Canvas assignments are authoritative for eligibility, roster sizes, alternates, required submissions, deadlines, and evaluation criteria. Cite those sources when recording requirements rather than creating new requirements here.

## Client objectives

- Summarize the business problem NAPA is asking the consulting team to address.
- Explain what makes the Olympic doubles-selection decisions strategically important.
- Identify the decisions NAPA expects to support with analytical evidence.
- Describe important business constraints, risks, and uncertainties.

## Recommendation scope

Record the team's understanding of the required recommendations:

- USA and Canada Olympic rosters;
- men's doubles, women's doubles, and mixed doubles selections;
- alternate teams; and
- development-camp recommendations.

Confirm exact eligibility, roster composition, and alternate requirements against the RFP and Canvas assignments. Record what is in scope, what is out of scope, and what remains unclear.

## Important business questions

- What business criteria should guide roster, alternate, and development-camp decisions?
- How should eligible doubles teams and partnership compatibility be evaluated and compared?
- What evidence should distinguish current Olympic readiness from development potential?
- How should data quality, limited history, uncertainty, and tradeoffs affect confidence?
- Which decisions require analytical evidence, and where is documented team judgment appropriate?
